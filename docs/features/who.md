# /watching: Watching now (was the /who preview)

> This is now the only design: the old sheet was removed (see `docs/features/watching-now.md`). `/who` redirects to `/watching`. Same auth as everything else.

**Idea.** No question box. Everything is a node (this title, a person, another title, a pair of people) reached by tapping; the one input is a finder that filters and finds. Desktop first: finder left (55%), node right (45%, own scroll) with a trail (`The Rookie S2E8 › Sara Rue › The Big Bang Theory`, Esc = back one step). Below 1000 px the panes stack: the details open over the finder with a Close button (top right of the trail bar), and a bottom dock (`Dock`) shows the step you were on so you can reopen them (nothing is lost by closing).

**Where things live**
- Pure, unit tested (`tests/unit/who-*.test.ts`): `lib/who-scope.ts` (scope + roster building, groups, gender/guest filters, finder), `who-dates.ts` (formatting, ages, gaps, readout, life line), `who-spans.ts` (a person's own episode dates), `who-timeline.ts` (axis layout), `who-common.ts` (titles in common, shared titles). Shapes in `who-types.ts`.
- Server: `lib/who-data.ts` (TMDB reads; `tmdb()` fetch cache + `cached()` data cache with the same fallback as `wiki.ts`; a small in-process map), `lib/who-api.ts` (input checks), routes `app/api/who/{roster,season,person,spans,known,shared,pair,title,common}` (GET, validated; signed-out requests get 401 JSON from `proxy.ts`).
- Client: `lib/who-client.ts` (session cache for every request, recent faces), `components/who/*` (`WhoApp` shell, `PositionControl`, `Finder`, `RootNode`, `PersonNode` + `Timeline`, `TitleNode`, `PairNode`), page `app/(app)/who/page.tsx`. CSS is the `/who` block at the end of `globals.css` (`wx-` prefix; keep class names unique: `.wx-top` is the top bar, `.wx-billed` a top-billed tile).

**Position.** Series only (films have none). `Pos {s, e}`, null = not sure. Saved in the `tc_watch` cookie when a watch for this title exists (`s`/`e` hold the nearest exact guess so the old sheet keeps working; the new optional `pos` field holds the true position when it is not an exact episode; the old sheet clears it when it sets an episode), always mirrored in `?k=tv-79744&s=2&e=8`. `/who` with no watch and no `k` shows a title search.

**Spoiler rule (do not weaken).** A person and their character appear only if TMDB says they appeared inside the scope. Exact S,E: season credits (`aggregate_credits` on the season call) for seasons before S, plus season S's per-episode `guest_stars` up to E and its regular cast (`credits`). Season only: every season <= S in full. Unknown season: the series' `aggregate_credits`, with a note. All seasons <= S are used, not the last six. A person's own dates for the show being watched are clipped to the position server-side (`spanFromEpisodes` with a scope), and that show's row is built only from what is in scope.

**Dates.** A series credit's date is the person's own first-to-last episode air date from `/credit/{id}`, never the show's start year. When TMDB's episode list is missing or shorter than their episode count the row says "during X's run, 2007-19" and shows no gap chip. Loaded lazily (visible rows first, 2 requests of 7 titles at a time); a skeleton shows until they arrive. A regular with no list gets "their first credited air date up to where you are". Gaps under a year read in months; 1-3 years "1 year 5 months"; longer, the nearest year.

**Known limits / decisions.** Roster capped at 2500 faces (lowest-ranked one-off guests of very long series are dropped). Regulars are the season's `credits.cast`; for an unknown season, billing order < 10 or at least half the most episodes. Talk/news/reality genres and "Self" roles are "extras", hidden unless the toggle is on. "With this cast" shows each person's dates as "from <first credited date>" for series (exact dates are on the pair node). Facts use `POST /api/ask` with `{k, s, e}` so they work without a watch; "Episodes left" / "This episode" are hidden unless the episode is exact.

## Polish round (calm and glanceable)
- **Finder order:** cast matches first; "Titles in common" sits under the faces, and with cast matches only for a strongly matching, popular title (`strongTitleMatch`, `titlesForQuery` in `who-common.ts`: 4+ letters, start of title or a whole word, TMDB popularity >= `MIN_POP`). With no cast match the panel is the answer. The Women/Men chip filters the people inside it.
- **Tiles:** 7 per row at 1440 (~96 px photos), one muted line "x4 · Character in Title" (gold = seen; the title is chosen by `pickSeenIn`, `src/lib/who-known.ts`, unit tested: your status, size of their part, episodes, fame of the title, whether you rated it; no character means "Seen in Title"), larger photo popover (fixed, hover/keyboard focus only, never touch). Nothing is drawn when nothing is known.
- **Timeline:** one compact row each (48 px on desktop, two short lines on a phone), 8 rows then "Show all N". The readout compares the reference only with a chosen row (`who-readout.ts`): hover/focus preview > click > default (title picked from "titles in common", else top "You know them from", else no readout). Axis: dots are a year or less, longer runs are bars, seen = green, selected = larger, tooltip on hover/focus, click scrolls to the row.
- **Dates:** own episode dates, else the seasons' air dates from TMDB's credit (`spanFromEpisodes` `seasons`), else the show's run "2020-22 (show's run)" with approximate gaps ("about 1 year after").
- `useSpans` returns a new `data` object on every update (memos downstream depend on it) and keeps other rows' dates when only the position changes.

## Improvement round (trail, history, spoilers, sticky timeline)
- **Trail and history** (`lib/who-trail.ts`, unit tested; `components/who/Trail.tsx`). The trail is the steps after the root plus a cursor. Clicking an earlier step only moves the cursor: later steps stay (dimmed) until a NEW step is opened from an earlier one, which replaces them and starts a new branch. Steps are fixed-size chips (round picture + one short line), so nothing shifts. "Recently opened" keeps one chip per branch (sessionStorage `typecast.who.recent2`, per title): only small round pictures, the breadcrumb `A > B > C` is the tooltip, clicking restores the whole trail. A person opened from the finder starts a new branch.
- **"This title"** is named `<title> SxEy` (`rootLabel`, `posCode`), has no section heading or "tap a face" hint; a film's poster is in its card. Every photo (top poster, episode still, title poster, film poster, person photos) opens the page-wide `Lightbox` (`useWho().zoom`, `ZoomBtn`).
- **Page-wide spoiler switch** (top bar, `role=switch`, label "No spoilers", on by default; shares `typecast.ask.spoilers` with the Watching bar). Off = spoilers allowed: the roster is the whole series, `clip` is null (a person's dates for this show are not cut at the position), facts get `spoilers: true`. The position still says where you are.
- **Episode chips** on a person open an `episode` node (`EpisodeNode`, shared `EpisodeCard`); they never change the position (that node has "Set S2E8 as where I am").
- **Timeline:** header, filters (one scrolling row), axis and readout are sticky under the trail (`.wx-tsticky`, `--wx-tl-h` is measured so row scroll margins clear it). What you are watching is gold: a bar of its own above the credits plus the show's own mark, drawn only as far as you know (its right end fades, tooltip "So far"). Close dots merge into one dot sized by count (`dotSize`; press steps through its credits; the watched show is `solo`, never merged). The chosen row keeps a gold border and faint gold wash (`.sel`); hover adds a lighter outline (`.hot`). The "measure from this row" button and the override are gone; each row and each "You know them from" title has the Glimpse button (About + images, spoiler-scoped, see `glimpse.md`) instead.
- **Finder:** All / Main cast / Guests (`byCast`).

## Round 3 (sticky fix, Next, history shortcuts)
- **Sticky timeline works:** its section class is `wx-tlsec` (the earlier `wx-tl` was also the trail label, with `overflow: hidden`, which stops `position: sticky` from working). The text readout under the axis, all relative-time chips ("7 years before this") and the actor's age are gone; each row shows the person's episode count for series ("4 episodes", the credit's count or what their own list adds up to) and a series' years in "You know them from" include the end ("2009-2017", "2018-" while it airs: `ShowRun` on `Span.show`, `yearsLabel`).
- **No spoilers switch:** off = switch off, text "No spoilers" (everything stops at where you are). Pressed = coloured, switch on, text "Show spoilers" (whole series). `aria-checked` is true when spoilers are shown.
- **Position control:** the episode picker and "Next" (next aired episode, else the next aired season's first) share one pill; each half has its own hover and pressed state.
- **Trail:** opening the step right before the one showing (only one back) is treated as Back (`pushNode`).

## Finder bar and Then photos (round 4)
- The search box and the All/Women/Men, All/Main/Guests, Now/Then chips sit in one sticky block (`.wx-stick`), so the chip bar stays visible under the search bar while the grid scrolls.
- "Then" photos are fetched ahead (and preloaded into the browser cache) for every face shown on an older title, whether or not the Now/Then switch is on. The hover pop (`Pop`) shows Now and Then side by side when a Then photo exists.

- **Why many faces got the same Then photo (Rookie S3E5):** the role search ("Actor as Character Show") returns IMDb's `title/ttX/characters/nmY` page for most guests, and the picture on those pages is the show's key art / the episode's still, the same for everyone; the scorer rated that page highest. Fixes: (1) for an exact episode the Then photo comes first from the show's Fandom episode gallery page (`<Episode>/Gallery`, captions "Actor as Character"; `getFandomEpisodeGallery` in `fandom.ts`, `parseGalleryCaptions`/`galleryFor` in `wiki-cast.ts`, cache `fandom-ep-gallery-v1`); the route takes `tv`, `s`, `e`, `imdb` for that. (2) `sharedThenIds` (`then-photo.ts`): a picture that several faces received is dropped for all of them. Only guests the wiki captions get a gallery still; the rest fall back to the search.

## Cameos from the wiki (Trivia lines)
TMDB, TVmaze and the Fandom Cast list all missed Mercedes Mason in The Rookie S3E10 (an uncredited speech); only the wiki's Trivia names her ("Captain [[Zoe Andersen]] ([[Mercedes Mason]]) makes an uncredited appearance"). `parseWikiCameos` (`src/lib/wiki-cast.ts`, unit tested) reads such lines (appearance word + actor in a linked "(…)") and `castOf` adds them to the wiki cast; they are matched back to TMDB people by name like other extra guests.
