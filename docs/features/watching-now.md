# Watching now (`/watching`)

> The old "Who's that?" sheet and its `/api/ask` GET routes were removed. Everything lives on the `/watching` page, described in `docs/features/who.md` (the file keeps its old name; code stays under `components/who`, `lib/who-*`, `/api/who/*`).

- "I'm watching this now" on a title stores the watch in the `tc_watch` cookie (this device, ends after 12h) and goes to `/watching`. The layout reads the cookie and draws the bar above the tabs (`components/Watching.tsx`): poster, "Watching now", title and where you are; tapping it opens `/watching`. It has no other button. `/watching?k=tv-79744&s=2&e=8` works with no watch at all; with no `k` and no watch it shows a title search.
- **The library keeps the watch** (pure decisions in `src/lib/watch-library.ts`, unit tested in `tests/unit/watch-library.test.ts`; no migration, it uses `status`, `progress_season`, `progress_episode`). Only the explicit start button counts as intent: opening /watching, searching, cast browsing, the season/episode pickers and the spoiler switch change nothing in the library.
  - Start (`start` in `WatchProvider`, via `startPlan`): a title that is missing, To watch or Partly watched becomes Watching through `useLib().mark` (toast "<Title>: added to Watching" with the normal Undo; the first time on this device, key `typecast.watchingToastSeen`, it adds "It will show in your Library."), and a series gets its place with `setProgress` once that save has landed. Watching and Watched titles keep their status (a rewatch stays Watched). What it was before goes in the watch as `prev` (`WatchPrev`: `st` none | watchlist | must | partial, plus the start place `s`/`e` for a series; absent when nothing was changed).
  - Stop watching (`stop`, via `restorePlan`): only when `prev` is set and the title is still Watching: Partly goes back to Partly, To watch / Must watch back to the Watchlist. A title that was not in the library is removed again only when no place beyond the start place was saved and it has no rating or note; otherwise it stays Watching. "Finished" on a film marks it Watched and does not restore (`stop({ restore: false })`). Saved progress is not cleared on restore.
  - Place: `/watching` with no `s`/`e` in the URL opens at this device's watch, else at the place saved in the library (`resumePlace`: Watching, Partly, or Watched with progress; never To watch), else not set (`openingPos`). While the title is Watching in the library, moving the place saves it with ONE debounced `setProgress` (800 ms trailing, flushed on page hide/unmount; `useSavedPlace`). A title that is not Watching is never written, and only exact episodes are saved. `/watching?k=<title>` for a Watching title works without a cookie (no cookie is created).
  - Empty `/watching`: "Pick up where you left off" above the search (`pickUpCards`, `WhoStart`): up to 6 Watching titles, most recently updated first, each linking to `/watching?k=..&s=..&e=..`. Hidden when there are none or the library cannot be read. CSS classes `wx-resume*`.
- A watch ends from the page ("End this watch" at the bottom of the title view: Stop watching, or for a film Finished: mark watched). A film's clock (`FilmClock` in `PositionControl.tsx`, the header slot where a series has its season/episode pickers) holds -5/+5 min and a play/pause button; pausing stores `paused` on the watch so the minutes stand still (`setPaused`/`shiftWatch`, `lib/watching.ts`). It feeds the "time left" fact.
- Facts: shown as four icon tabs (Story, Making, Reception, Watching; `FACT_GROUPS` in `lib/watching.ts`, `RootNode.tsx`); the open tab loads its facts by itself, so nothing is tapped one by one. `POST /api/ask` (`lib/answer.ts`, `lib/ask-intent.ts`, sources TMDB and Wikidata/Wikipedia via `lib/wiki.ts`, plot sections skipped unless spoilers are shown). Only the POST remains; the page names its own title and position in the body, so a watch is not needed.
- `/who` is a redirect to `/watching` that keeps the query string.
- Person page: the titles under the timeline are ordered by recognition, not date (`rankByRecognition`, `src/lib/who-known.ts`, unit tested): the same signals as the "Seen in" pick (size of their part, billing, episodes, fame of the title, a boost for what you have seen/rated). The show being watched leads; ties go to the newer title. The axis above still draws the dates.
- With spoilers shown on an exact episode, the Finder keeps that episode's cast but `WhoApp` fetches the whole-series roster for `faces` (shared-cast lists on title/person/pair nodes).
- A refresh keeps your place: the trail (steps, cursor, details open) is saved per title in sessionStorage (`typecast.watching.trail.<key>`, `cleanSession` in `lib/who-trail.ts`) and put back on mount; the Back-gesture depth is re-adopted from `history.state.wxDepth` so no extra history entries are pushed.
- Finder density: on windows 560px or wider the face photos are capped at `(100svh - 445px) / 2` (floor 100px, `object-position: 50% 20%`) so two full rows of tiles, including the character and "known from" lines, fit on a short window; "Recently opened" sits on one line with its label. Tall windows and portrait phones keep the plain 2:3 photos (rules at the end of `globals.css`).
- Missing guest stars: TMDB's episode cast has gaps (Daniel Lissing absent from The Rookie S3E7; Shawn Ashmore, a regular that season, absent from S3E7 and S3E8). For an exact episode, `fillGuests` (`src/lib/who-data.ts`) merges two more sources for that episode and up to 12 earlier ones in the season: TVmaze's guest cast (`src/lib/tvmaze.ts`: show by IMDb id, `/episodes/<id>/guestcast`) and the show's Fandom wiki episode page (`getFandomEpisodeCast` in `src/lib/fandom.ts`, pure parser `src/lib/wiki-cast.ts`: every sub-section of the page's Cast heading, Main included; the page must say it belongs to that season). New names are matched to TMDB people by exact name (most popular on a tie) so faces keep their person page, and show under "In this episode". Fails soft to TMDB alone. Earlier seasons still use TMDB's season credits, so gaps there are not filled. IMDb itself cannot be used: its bot protection blocks server requests. Cache key `who-roster-v3`.
- Person node header has a "Full profile" `PersonLink` that opens the regular person popup (bio, all credits, photos) over /watching.
- Title details: the watched title in the top bar is a `TitleLink` (a trailing ›) that opens the regular title popup over /watching; it adds no height.

- Back after reopening a "Recently opened" line or tapping the phone Details dock: reopening pushes ONE history entry (`single`/`lo` refs in `WhoApp`), so Back closes the details instead of walking back through the restored trail. Trails you build step by step still get one entry per step.
- Phone layout (< 560px, end of `globals.css`): the header is two slim rows (poster, title, No spoilers switch, icon-only Close; then season and episode pickers). Once the face list is scrolled, `WhoApp` sets `.wx[data-compact]` (hysteresis 72px down / 12px up) and the header drops to one row (poster, title, switch icon, Close). Search and ONE single row of filters (Gender, Cast, Now/Then, all of which must fit at 390px; the count is hidden) stay pinned in `.wx-stick`. Keep the Close and switch `aria-label`s (their text is hidden on phones). Screenshot the Now/Then switch with `scripts/shot.mjs --mock '**/api/glimpse/then*={"configured":true,"url":null}'` (it hides itself when image search is not configured, as in the sandbox).

- **Title switcher** (header of `/watching`, `components/who/TitleSwitcher.tsx`, rows from pure `switchCards` in `src/lib/watch-library.ts`, unit tested): a small quiet chevron (▾, "Switch title") right after the title link, shown only when the library has at least one OTHER Watching title. It adds no height (its tap target is a `::before`; the title sits in `.wx-trl`, a flex row) and works in the phone two-row and compact one-row headers. It opens a light popover under the header (`.wx-tswm`, not a sheet): up to 6 other Watching titles, most recently updated first, each poster + title + saved place ("S3 E8", nothing for films), linking to `/watching?k=..&s=..&e=..`; never episode titles or plots. Last row "Search another title…" goes to `/watching?search=1`, which shows the empty-state search (`WhoStart`) even while a watch exists. Esc closes and returns focus to the chevron; a click outside closes. The rows are a server prop (`others`, from the same cached `getLibrary()` read as the saved place, run beside `loadBrief`, no extra TMDB call); any failure means no chevron. Switching changes nothing: not the library, not the `tc_watch` cookie (it is only rewritten when the place moves on the title the watch is for). Smoke: `behaviour.spec.ts` "title switcher".
