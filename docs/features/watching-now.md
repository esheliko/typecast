# Watching now (`/watching`)

> The old "Who's that?" sheet and its `/api/ask` GET routes were removed. Everything lives on the `/watching` page, described in `docs/features/who.md` (the file keeps its old name; code stays under `components/who`, `lib/who-*`, `/api/who/*`).

- "I'm watching this now" on a title stores the watch in the `tc_watch` cookie (this device, ends after 12h) and goes to `/watching`. The layout reads the cookie and draws the bar above the tabs (`components/Watching.tsx`): poster, "Watching now", title and where you are; tapping it opens `/watching`. It has no other button. `/watching?k=tv-79744&s=2&e=8` works with no watch at all; with no `k` and no watch it shows a title search.
- **The library keeps the watch** (pure decisions in `src/lib/watch-library.ts`, unit tested in `tests/unit/watch-library.test.ts`; no migration, it uses `status`, `progress_season`, `progress_episode`). Opening /watching, searching, cast browsing, the pickers and the spoiler switch never change a title's STATUS; only the explicit start button and the real "I am watching this" signals below do.
  - **Two meanings, one mapping.** The page place (`s`/`e` in the URL, the watch cookie, the pickers) is the episode being watched NOW. The library's progress is "watched up to and including" (`upTo`, `seenIn`, `setWatchedUpTo`; see `docs/features/series.md`). So progress = the episode BEFORE the place (`placeToProgress`, uses `prevEpisode` and the season sizes in the brief; S1 E1, specials and an unknown previous season write nothing) and the place to continue with = the episode AFTER the progress (`continuePlace`; with season sizes it rolls into the next season, without them it is `e + 1` and the page runs `normalizePlace`). Never save the episode you are on as progress: its cast would count as seen and "continue" would skip it.
  - Start (`start`/`engage` in `WatchProvider`, via `startPlan`): a title that is missing, To watch or Partly watched becomes Watching through `useLib().mark` (toast "<Title>: added to Watching" with the normal Undo; the first time on this device, key `typecast.watchingToastSeen`, it adds "It will show in your Library."). The watch starts at the episode after the saved progress (S1 E1 with none) and writes NO progress (nothing is finished yet). Watching and Watched titles keep their status (a rewatch stays Watched). What it was before goes in the watch as `prev` (`WatchPrev`: `st` none | watchlist | must | partial, plus the start place `s`/`e` for a series; absent when nothing was changed).
  - **Signals** (`signal` in the watch context, same function as the start button without the navigation): Next, the film clock's play (when resuming) and its -5/+5 min buttons count as "I am watching this". A title that is not Watching moves to Watching, the watch cookie is created or updated, `prev` is remembered and the same toast shows (with Undo). Watched titles keep their status. Pickers, opening the page, search and the spoiler switch are never signals.
  - **Next** (`PositionControl`, `useSavedPlace().next`): "I finished this episode": the episode you were on becomes the saved progress (ONE write via `setProgress`, only when beyond the saved progress; a Watched title with no progress is left alone, one with progress only moves forward) and the toast says "S3 E8 marked watched" with Undo (puts the previous progress back; with none, it clears it). On the final episode of a series TMDB says has Ended/Canceled the button stays enabled, marks that episode and shows "That was the last episode. Mark the series watched?" (dismissible, once per watch, `typecast.watchPrompt.<key>`, cleared when the watch ends). An airing series shows nothing extra.
  - **Film finished prompt**: when the film clock reaches the runtime a quiet "Finished? Mark watched" line shows under the clock (dismissible, once per watch, never automatic; Mark watched = Watched + end the watch without restoring).
  - **Pickers are forward-only** (`forwardSave`, `useSavedPlace().place`): while the title is Watching, moving the season/episode may only advance the saved progress; going back or staying saves nothing (checking an earlier episode's cast never rolls progress back; lowering progress is an explicit action in the title popup's checklist). One debounced write (800 ms, flushed on page hide/unmount). A move more than one episode beyond the saved place says "Place saved: S3 E12" with Undo; a single step is silent. A title that is not Watching is never written by the pickers.
  - **Partly reverts quietly**: when a start or signal promoted a title from Partly watched, the device remembers `typecast.watchPrev.<key>` = `{prev: "partial", lastActive, type, id, title, year, poster}` (`src/lib/watch-prev.ts`, every storage call in try/catch). `lastActive` is refreshed by place saves, Next and signals. When the app loads (`WatchProvider`, client only, 1.5 s after mount), `revertDecision` puts a record older than 12 h back to Partly watched when no watch is active and the title is still Watching (toast "<Title> went back to Partly watched"); records for titles whose status changed meanwhile, or older than 30 days, are dropped. Best effort and device-local; no migration.
  - Stop watching (`stop`, via `restorePlan`): only when `prev` is set and the title is still Watching: Partly goes back to Partly, To watch / Must watch back to the Watchlist. A title that was not in the library is removed again only when no place beyond the start place was saved and it has no rating or note; otherwise it stays Watching. "Finished" on a film marks it Watched and does not restore (`stop({ restore: false })`). Saved progress is not cleared on restore.
  - Opening place: `/watching` with no `s`/`e` in the URL opens at this device's watch, else at the episode after the library's progress (`resumePlace`: Watching, Partly, or Watched with progress; never To watch), else not set (`openingPos`). A place past the end of its season (a link built without season sizes) rolls over via `normalizePlace`. `/watching?k=<title>` for a Watching title works without a cookie (no cookie is created). The server passes the library's progress to `WhoApp` (`savedProgress`) as the baseline for the forward-only rule; it is tracked locally afterwards, so progress lowered in a title popup while /watching stays open is not noticed until the next load.
  - Empty `/watching`: "Pick up where you left off" above the search (`pickUpCards`, `WhoStart`): up to 6 Watching titles, most recently updated first, each linking to `/watching?k=..&s=..&e=..` at the episode to CONTINUE with (label "S3 E9" for progress S3 E8; without TMDB the card cannot know a season ended, so its label is `e + 1` and the page rolls it over). Hidden when there are none or the library cannot be read. CSS classes `wx-resume*`.
- A watch ends from the page ("End this watch" at the bottom of the title view: Stop watching, or for a film Finished: mark watched). A film's clock (`FilmClock` in `PositionControl.tsx`, the header slot where a series has its season/episode pickers) holds -5/+5 min and a play/pause button (play and +-5 are signals, see above); pausing stores `paused` on the watch so the minutes stand still (`setPaused`/`shiftWatch`, `lib/watching.ts`). It feeds the "time left" fact.
- Facts: shown in three groups (The story, Behind the scenes, Verdict and viewing; `FACT_GROUPS` in `lib/watching.ts`) so a detail is found by topic. `POST /api/ask` (`lib/answer.ts`, `lib/ask-intent.ts`, sources TMDB and Wikidata/Wikipedia via `lib/wiki.ts`, plot sections skipped unless spoilers are shown). Only the POST remains; the page names its own title and position in the body, so a watch is not needed.
- `/who` is a redirect to `/watching` that keeps the query string.
- Person page: the titles under the timeline are ordered by recognition, not date (`rankByRecognition`, `src/lib/who-known.ts`, unit tested): the same signals as the "Seen in" pick (size of their part, billing, episodes, fame of the title, a boost for what you have seen/rated). The show being watched leads; ties go to the newer title. The axis above still draws the dates.
- With spoilers shown on an exact episode, the Finder keeps that episode's cast but `WhoApp` fetches the whole-series roster for `faces` (shared-cast lists on title/person/pair nodes).
- A refresh keeps your place: the trail (steps, cursor, details open) is saved per title in sessionStorage (`typecast.watching.trail.<key>`, `cleanSession` in `lib/who-trail.ts`) and put back on mount; the Back-gesture depth is re-adopted from `history.state.wxDepth` so no extra history entries are pushed.
- Finder density: on windows 560px or wider the face photos are capped at `(100svh - 445px) / 2` (floor 100px, `object-position: 50% 20%`) so two full rows of tiles, including the character and "known from" lines, fit on a short window; "Recently opened" sits on one line with its label. Tall windows and portrait phones keep the plain 2:3 photos (rules at the end of `globals.css`).
- Missing guest stars: TMDB's episode cast has gaps (Daniel Lissing absent from The Rookie S3E7; Shawn Ashmore, a regular that season, absent from S3E7 and S3E8). For an exact episode, `fillGuests` (`src/lib/who-data.ts`) merges two more sources for that episode and up to 12 earlier ones in the season: TVmaze's guest cast (`src/lib/tvmaze.ts`: show by IMDb id, `/episodes/<id>/guestcast`) and the show's Fandom wiki episode page (`getFandomEpisodeCast` in `src/lib/fandom.ts`, pure parser `src/lib/wiki-cast.ts`: every sub-section of the page's Cast heading, Main included; the page must say it belongs to that season). New names are matched to TMDB people by exact name (most popular on a tie) so faces keep their person page, and show under "In this episode". Fails soft to TMDB alone. Earlier seasons still use TMDB's season credits, so gaps there are not filled. IMDb itself cannot be used: its bot protection blocks server requests. Cache key `who-roster-v3`.
- Person node header has a "Full profile" `PersonLink` that opens the regular person popup (bio, all credits, photos) over /watching.
- Title details: the watched title in the top bar is a `TitleLink` (a trailing ›) that opens the regular title popup over /watching; it adds no height.

- Back after reopening a "Recently opened" line or tapping the phone Details dock: reopening pushes ONE history entry (`single`/`lo` refs in `WhoApp`), so Back closes the details instead of walking back through the restored trail. Trails you build step by step still get one entry per step.
- Phone layout (< 560px, end of `globals.css`): the header is two slim rows (poster, title, No spoilers switch, icon-only Close; then season and episode pickers). Once the face list is scrolled, `WhoApp` sets `.wx[data-compact]` (hysteresis 72px down / 12px up) and the header drops to one row (poster, title, switch icon, Close). Search and ONE single row of filters (Gender, Cast, Now/Then, all of which must fit at 390px; the count is hidden) stay pinned in `.wx-stick`. Keep the Close and switch `aria-label`s (their text is hidden on phones). Screenshot the Now/Then switch with `scripts/shot.mjs --mock '**/api/glimpse/then*={"configured":true,"url":null}'` (it hides itself when image search is not configured, as in the sandbox).

- **Title switcher** (header of `/watching`, `components/who/TitleSwitcher.tsx`, rows from pure `switchCards` in `src/lib/watch-library.ts`, unit tested): a small quiet chevron (▾, "Switch title") right after the title link, shown only when the library has at least one OTHER Watching title. It adds no height (its tap target is a `::before`; the title sits in `.wx-trl`, a flex row) and works in the phone two-row and compact one-row headers. It opens a light popover under the header (`.wx-tswm`, not a sheet): up to 6 other Watching titles, most recently updated first, each poster + title + the place to continue with ("S3 E9" for progress S3 E8, nothing for films), linking to `/watching?k=..&s=..&e=..`; never episode titles or plots. Last row "Search another title…" goes to `/watching?search=1`, which shows the empty-state search (`WhoStart`) even while a watch exists. Esc closes and returns focus to the chevron; a click outside closes. The rows are a server prop (`others`, from the same cached `getLibrary()` read as the saved place, run beside `loadBrief`, no extra TMDB call); any failure means no chevron. Switching changes nothing: not the library, not the `tc_watch` cookie (it is only rewritten when the place moves on the title the watch is for). Smoke: `behaviour.spec.ts` "title switcher", "Next marks the episode you were on as watched, with Undo" (scratch title Band of Brothers).
