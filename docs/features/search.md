# Search

> Moved verbatim out of CLAUDE.md so sessions only read it when they touch this feature.

- Search results (`components/Search.tsx`, `/api/search`): `layoutSearch` (`src/lib/search-intent.ts`, pure, unit tested) decides whether a query is about a title, a person, both or neither (name-match tier with a leading "the/a/an" ignored, TMDB popularity/votes with separate cut-offs for people and titles in `CUT`) and returns the Top result (opened by Enter), the other kind, People and titles, with weak matches folded. People under popularity 1 are hidden unless the full name was typed; marked titles lead only among real name matches, and a marked title that matches as well as the best one becomes the Top result. The API asks `spell.ts` for a spelling fix only when the intent is unclear and uses it only if it gives a clear answer (`exact=1` turns it off). Short queries also fetch page 2 of TMDB's movie/TV search (La La Land is on page 2 of "la la"). Recent searches are kept per device (`tc:recent-searches`), saved when a result is opened.

Collections ("James Bond Collection") come from `searchCollections` with the pop/votes of their biggest film; `pickCollections` shows them below the Top result and hides an unknown franchise (under 1000 votes and popularity 20) unless you have marked one of its films or nothing else matched ("rookie" must open The Rookie, not a Japanese film series).

## Who's that? tab
Who's that? is the third tab next to Browse and Connections on the home page (the URL is `?view=who`, the old `?m=who` still opens it; nothing else stored). The search box has no switcher and no People-only mode (`?m=people` is gone). Who's that? (`components/WhoSearch.tsx`, pure `src/lib/who-search.ts`, unit tested) takes a title (Watching series first, or any title via `/api/search?kind=title`), a place (`PositionPicker`, default `startPos`: the episode after the saved place of a Watching series, whole series otherwise, none for a film) and "A name or role you remember". TMDB has no plot text, so only the actor's name and the character match (`matchWho`: every typed word starts a word of the name or role; name first, then role; this episode, main cast, earlier, guests). The people come from `/api/who/roster` (cut server-side at the place, never a later episode) and open with `PersonLink`. Failures show a small note.

(No-title search also: a character with several casts ("sheldon cooper mom") is followed into the other shows its actors play it in, so both Mary Coopers show up; character ranks and jobs TMDB lacks ("sergent rookie" -> Wade Grey, "A sergeant (later lieutenant)") come from the first sentence of each character in the Wikipedia article's cast section (`parseCastBlurbs`; skipped for a series held back by a spoiler line; abbreviations like Sgt. count). Also: a title can sit anywhere in the text, be cut short (two words or more) or be its initials ("himym"); what is left then matches a character or actor name of that title, a relative of a character from names, gender and the actors' birth years ("big bang sheldon mom" -> Mary Cooper, not his sister), or an episode name ("asian restaurant" -> The Chinese Restaurant; never for a Watching series under a spoiler line); when TMDB finds nothing, Wikipedia's spelling of the whole text is tried once ("sienfeld"). Also: the actor's name may be misspelt and sit anywhere in the text; a title then a character ("hunger games haymitch") shows that title's cast; and one cut-short or misspelt name word ("radclif wizard") picks the most popular match, tried last. The tab's wrapper has `data-no-lift` so focusing its inputs does not scroll the page (`KeyboardAware`).)

With **no title chosen (the default; the tab no longer pre-picks a Watching series)** the box takes an actor and a part or title: `/api/who/find` finds the person whose full name starts the text, then `matchCredits` (`src/lib/who-actor.ts`, unit tested) keeps their credits where every leftover word starts a word of the character or the title ("emmy rossum fiona", "kate winslet spotless mind"; filler like "as" is dropped, and a few role words such as lawyer/criminal also match close character words like attorney/convict). TMDB has no role descriptions, so when no character fits, `/api/who/find` also reads the TMDB keywords of their 40 best-known titles (`themes`, ranked last, `by: "theme"`: the story fits, which does not prove they play that part): "as criminal" finds heist/prison/mafia titles, "as lawyer" courtroom/legal ones. Held-back series carry no episode counts.

**Relatives and characters, v2.** A character with no show named ("ted sister" -> Heather Mosby, Ted Mosby's sister) is looked for in the casts of the 80 most-voted series (`famousRelation`, last resort after the search's own titles and a partial actor name; the show where the character is billed highest wins, so Ted Mosby beats Ted Wheeler). `relatives` ignores a "Sr." role for a sibling, counts a TMDB gender-3 actor only when nobody of the right gender fits, drops actors whose birth year is unknown when someone's known age fits, and an actor ruled out by age in one show (the father George Cooper) is ruled out in the next too ("sheldon brother" -> Georgie only). A one-word actor ("LISA") does not beat a named title that has that character ("himym lisa" -> the Lisas of HIMYM). Every row that is a character (title cast, relatives, an actor's matching roles) has a Glimpse button beside it (`.whs-rowg`; `castSpec` / `creditSpec`), never inside the link.

A title alone ("harry potter") answers with the most popular title that starts with those words and its main cast, characters named in the text first (`mainCast`, `popularTitle`; `plain: true` in the answer). The main search box (`Search.tsx`) also asks `/api/who/find` for queries of two words or more and shows the same answer (`who/FindAnswer.tsx`, shared with the tab) under "Who's that?" above the Top result, unless the answer is `plain` (a bare name or title the normal results already show).

## Summary rule (moved from CLAUDE.md)

- Search results are laid out by pure `layoutSearch` (`src/lib/search-intent.ts`, unit tested): Top result, other kind, weak matches folded; spell-fix only when intent is unclear. Search has a "Who's that?" mode (`components/WhoSearch.tsx`, pure `src/lib/who-search.ts`: find cast by a name or role you remember, cut at your place).
