# Upcoming and calendar

> Moved verbatim out of CLAUDE.md so sessions only read it when they touch this feature.

- Upcoming (Library, `components/Upcoming.tsx`) lists unreleased films (To watch) and the next episode of every series in the library. Pure logic in `src/lib/upcoming.ts` (unit tested: candidates, dates, grouping); `loadUpcoming` (`src/lib/upcoming-data.ts`) asks TMDB about at most 40 titles behind Suspense and fails soft (renders nothing). Films whose saved year is before this year are never looked up. A film more than 120 days out shows its month only. Title popup/page shows `Releases <date>` for films (`Title.release`); series already show "Next episode". v2: film dates are per country (`release-dates.ts`: TMDB `release_dates`, theatrical, then digital, then physical/TV, else the primary date; `Title.releaseBy`). Title popup has a small "+ Calendar" link to `/api/event?key=movie:603` (one all-day .ics, date looked up server-side; `ical.ts` is the pure writer: escaping, folding, stable UIDs). Library rows get a quiet `pill when` badge ("Out Fri", "S5 in 12 days", `rowBadge`) fed by `Upcoming` through a browser-side store (`upcoming-badges.ts`, `RowBadge.tsx`). Feed: `profiles.calendar_token` (migration 0009, stored as-is so Settings can show it again) is read only by the `calendar_library(tok)` security-definer function; `/cal/<token>.ics` (`src/app/cal/[file]/route.ts`, `/cal` is open in `proxy.ts`) takes the token and nothing else, answers a plain 404 for anything wrong (fail closed, also before the migration runs), and Settings has the `CalendarFeed` card (hidden until the column exists; Copy / Subscribe / Reset). Never log the token or put it in a URL other than that route.

## Upcoming picks ("You may like", Browse "Upcoming")

- Unreleased films and new series NOT in the library that fit your taste. Pure ranking in `src/lib/upcoming-rank.ts` (unit tested): genre/theme/language fit with your For you profile, people you like (noisy-or), +0.45 for the next part of a film saga you liked, a little TMDB popularity ("hype"), minus fit with what you disliked. No quality term: unreleased titles have no votes.
- Data in `src/lib/upcoming-picks.ts` (`upcomingPicks`): seeds are your 12 strongest library titles (same weights and detail cache as For you), candidates come from TMDB discover (due tomorrow..365 days, your top genres, two pages + most anticipated overall), the coming credits of your 6 strongest people, and the parts of your liked films' collections. The ranking is memoised per user/kind/library state for 10 minutes, so Browse pages and the Library share it.
- Library: `libraryPicks` gives the best 6 across both kinds above `MIN_PICK`, shown as a "You may like" group at the bottom of the Upcoming panel (with "See all" to Browse). It gets a 2.5 s budget and fails soft; picks are not counted in the header number and never feed the row badges.
- Browse: the "Upcoming" pill (`list=upcoming`, `/api/browse`, `upcomingPage`) pages through the same ranking; cards show "Out 14 Dec" (`dueLabel`) instead of the year. "Coming soon" stays as the generic most-anticipated list (its cards now show the release day too); with nothing to learn from, Upcoming falls back to it with a note.
