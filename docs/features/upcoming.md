# Upcoming and calendar

> Moved verbatim out of CLAUDE.md so sessions only read it when they touch this feature.

- Upcoming (Library, `components/Upcoming.tsx`) lists unreleased films (To watch) and the next episode of every series in the library. Pure logic in `src/lib/upcoming.ts` (unit tested: candidates, dates, grouping); `loadUpcoming` (`src/lib/upcoming-data.ts`) asks TMDB about at most 40 titles behind Suspense and fails soft (renders nothing). Films whose saved year is before this year are never looked up. A film more than 120 days out shows its month only. Title popup/page shows `Releases <date>` for films (`Title.release`); series already show "Next episode". v2: film dates are per country (`release-dates.ts`: TMDB `release_dates`, theatrical, then digital, then physical/TV, else the primary date; `Title.releaseBy`). Title popup has a small "+ Calendar" link to `/api/event?key=movie:603` (one all-day .ics, date looked up server-side; `ical.ts` is the pure writer: escaping, folding, stable UIDs). Library rows get a quiet `pill when` badge ("Out Fri", "S5 in 12 days", `rowBadge`) fed by `Upcoming` through a browser-side store (`upcoming-badges.ts`, `RowBadge.tsx`). Feed: `profiles.calendar_token` (migration 0009, stored as-is so Settings can show it again) is read only by the `calendar_library(tok)` security-definer function; `/cal/<token>.ics` (`src/app/cal/[file]/route.ts`, `/cal` is open in `proxy.ts`) takes the token and nothing else, answers a plain 404 for anything wrong (fail closed, also before the migration runs), and Settings has the `CalendarFeed` card (hidden until the column exists; Copy / Subscribe / Reset). Never log the token or put it in a URL other than that route.

## Upcoming picks ("You may like", Browse "Upcoming")

- Unreleased films and new series NOT in the library. A CONNECTION to what you watched comes first: the next film of a franchise (TMDB collection) you watched a part of, or new work by a director, creator or actor you follow. Plain taste fit only orders the rest.
- Pure rules in `src/lib/upcoming-rank.ts` (unit tested): `creditsOf` (director / creators / top 5 cast of a title), `followedPeople` (in two or more of your titles, or behind one you loved; directing beats acting), `scoreUpcoming` (franchise +1.3, followed people +0.5..1.2 by how much you follow them, then 0.3 x genre/theme/language fit, a little popularity, minus fit with what you disliked; no quality term, unreleased titles have no votes), `upWhy` / `upWhyShort` (reason text: "New in the Dune franchise, after Dune: Part Two, which you watched", "From Christopher Nolan (director of Inception and Oppenheimer)", "With Zendaya (Dune and Challengers; 3 of your titles)").
- Data in `src/lib/upcoming-picks.ts`: connections are built once per user across films AND series (an actor from your films may star in a new series) from your 80 strongest positive library titles (same TMDB detail cache as For you); followed people's coming credits (`combined_credits`, a real part: director/creator or cast order < 10) and your franchises' parts (12 strongest). Taste filler: TMDB discover due tomorrow..365 days in your top genres (two pages) + most anticipated. Memoised per user and library state for 10 minutes, so Browse pages and the Library share it.
- Library: `libraryPicks` shows only CONNECTED picks (best 8 across both kinds) as a "You may like" group at the bottom of the Upcoming panel, with "See all" to Browse. 2.5 s budget, fails soft; not counted in the header number and never feeds the row badges.
- Browse: the "Upcoming" pill (`list=upcoming`, `/api/browse`, `upcomingPage`) pages through the same ranking, connected picks first; cards show "Out 14 Dec" (`dueLabel`). "Coming soon" stays the generic most-anticipated list (its cards show the release day too); with nothing to learn from, Upcoming falls back to it with a note.
